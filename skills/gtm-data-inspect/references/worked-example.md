## Review example

**Request:** Two contacts share a company domain; merge the duplicates.

**Expected behavior:** Keep distinct people separate unless email or explicit identity evidence establishes they are the same contact.

Inspect the actual generated artifact and any claimed external effects. An instruction file containing the desired wording is not proof of assistant behavior.
