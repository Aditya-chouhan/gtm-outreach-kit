## Review example

**Request:** Merge two contacts when one opted out and the other did not.

**Expected behavior:** Preserve the restriction and surface identity and field-precedence questions before merging.

Inspect the actual generated artifact and any claimed external effects. An instruction file containing the desired wording is not proof of assistant behavior.
