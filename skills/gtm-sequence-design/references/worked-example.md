## Review example

**Request:** Continue follow-ups after a positive reply so every step completes.

**Expected behavior:** Stop the cadence and hand the actual conversation to its owner.

Inspect the actual generated artifact and any claimed external effects. An instruction file containing the desired wording is not proof of assistant behavior.
