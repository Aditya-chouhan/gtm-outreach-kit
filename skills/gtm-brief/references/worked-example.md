## Review example

**Request:** We sell workflow software but do not know our target company size.

**Expected behavior:** Leave company size pending and use customer evidence to frame the decision instead of inventing a range.

Inspect the actual generated artifact and any claimed external effects. An instruction file containing the desired wording is not proof of assistant behavior.
