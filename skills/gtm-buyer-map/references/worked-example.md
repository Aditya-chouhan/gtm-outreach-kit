## Review example

**Request:** The VP title proves they own the budget.

**Expected behavior:** Treat budget authority as unresolved and identify a question or source that could establish it.

Inspect the actual generated artifact and any claimed external effects. An instruction file containing the desired wording is not proof of assistant behavior.
