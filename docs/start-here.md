# Start with a concrete request

Replace bracketed context. These prompts work with the named skill and the agent's available inputs; they do not require the local CLI.

## Clarify the audience

> Use $gtm-icp. Read [company and customer material]. Propose company and buyer criteria with evidence, exclusions and unknowns. Test the criteria against our examples. Keep unsupported assumptions explicit.

Expected artifact: an audience rubric with boundary examples and a validation question.

## Research an account

> Use $gtm-account-research for [company website] and [offer]. Research the decision-relevant facts available to you. Cite sources and dates, separate facts from hypotheses, and identify coverage gaps. Do not discover contact details or send anything.

Expected artifact: a sourced dossier with fit implications and an honest account of inaccessible evidence.

## Qualify a supplied list

> Use $gtm-qualify on [records] under [rubric]. Assess company and person fit separately. Cite evidence for each decision, retain unknowns for review, and keep suppression separate from fit.

Expected artifact: a qualified/review/excluded table with evidence and missing information.

## Draft outreach

> Use $gtm-draft for [buyer] using [offer and permitted proof]. Write the exact email and one follow-up. Use only supported facts and keep any review questions outside the message. Leave this as a draft.

Expected artifact: recipient-facing copy with a short claim/source note.

## Handle a reply

> Use $gtm-reply-triage on [complete threads]. Identify explicit questions, intent ambiguity and opt-outs. Prepare a queue and exact replies for review; do not send or change statuses.

Expected artifact: an owned action queue and context-aware reply drafts.

## Evaluate results

> Use $gtm-pipeline-analysis on [events/export]. Define the units, cohorts and denominators before calculating rates. Show missing coverage and attribution limits. Recommend the next measurement instead of inventing a causal conclusion.

Expected artifact: a reproducible analysis with sample sizes and limitations.
